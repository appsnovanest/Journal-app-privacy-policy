Summary

My Diary is a personal journal. The entries, moods and images you create are yours. We do not sell your journal, and we never use what you write for advertising. The free version of the app shows ads from Google AdMob; My Diary Pro removes them. Apart from advertising, the only diagnostic data we collect is crash reports, so we can fix bugs.

  • Your written entries, mood logs, photos and drawings are stored in your own private area of Google Firebase, readable only by your signed-in account.
  • Ads never see your journal entries, moods, images or PIN.
  • In the UK, EEA and Switzerland we ask for your consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • You can export everything, delete everything, or delete your whole account from inside the app at any time.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy explains how the Android application My Diary: Daily Journal (package com.novanest.journalapp, “the app”, “we”, “us”) handles information about you. It applies only to this app. It does not cover any other app, website or service.

By using the app you agree to the handling of information described here. If you do not agree, please stop using the app and delete it.

2. What we collect

2.1 Account information

Signing in is handled by Firebase Authentication. You can sign in with Google or with an email address and password. Either way we receive:

  • your email address;
  • your display name (from your Google account, or the name you enter);
  • a Firebase user ID, which is what your journal is filed under.

We never see your Google password. If you sign in with email, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication, which stores it only in hashed form; we cannot read it. Your display name is also saved on your device so the app can greet you without a network call.

2.2 Content you create

Everything you choose to write or record in the app:

  • journal entries — title, body text, tags, and the favourite flag;
  • the mood you attach to an entry, and the mood you log for a day;
  • a place name, if you type one. This is a free-text field you fill in yourself. The app does not read your device's GPS or location services;
  • the date and time an entry was created and last edited.

2.3 Photos and drawings

When you attach a photo, set a cover image, or make a drawing, the image is copied into the app's private internal storage on your device. A compressed copy is backed up to Google Cloud Firestore, under your account, where only your signed-in account can read it. This is what lets your images come back when you reinstall the app or move to a new phone.

The app asks for a photo only at the moment you tap to add one, through the Android photo picker. It has no standing access to your gallery. If you choose to save a drawing to your gallery, the app writes only that image.

2.4 Settings stored on your device

The following stay on your phone and are not sent to us:

  • your screen-lock PIN for the app, stored only as a salted one-way hash;
  • whether you enabled unlocking with biometrics;
  • your daily reminder time and whether reminders are on;
  • your theme and font choices;
  • whether My Diary Pro is active, and the free-plan counters (for example, how many times you changed today's mood, or whether you watched an ad to unlock an extra entry today);
  • your cached display name.

If you enable biometric unlock, authentication is performed entirely by Android's own biometric system. The app never receives, sees or stores your fingerprint or face data.

2.5 Crash reports

If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains technical details only: the error and where in the app's code it happened, your device model, Android version, app version, and a random installation identifier. Crash reports never include your journal entries, mood logs, images or PIN, and they are not linked to your name or email address.

3. Advertising

The free version of the app shows a banner ad, and offers optional “rewarded” ads you can choose to watch to unlock a little extra free use. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. My Diary Pro subscribers see no ads, and the ad service is not started for them.

3.1 What the ad service collects

To show, measure and protect ads against fraud, Google AdMob collects information from your device, which may include:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate (city- or region-level) location may be inferred;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as whether an ad was shown, tapped or completed.

We never pass your journal entries, moods, images, tags, place names, email address or PIN to the ad service, and ads are not chosen based on what you write.

3.2 Consent and your choices

  • UK, EEA and Switzerland — before any ad is requested, the app shows Google's consent message, where you can accept, reject or customise personalised advertising. If you do not consent to personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Everywhere — you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (on most devices: Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Remove ads entirely by subscribing to My Diary Pro.

How Google uses information from apps that show its ads is explained at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

US residents: showing personalised ads using your advertising ID may count as “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under some US state privacy laws. You can opt out by deleting your advertising ID or turning off ad personalisation in your phone's settings as described above, or by contacting us.

4. My Diary Pro subscription

My Diary Pro is an optional subscription sold through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive your card or other payment details.

When you subscribe or restore a purchase, Google Play tells the app which subscription you own and whether it is active, together with a purchase token. The app uses this only to unlock Pro features and remove ads, and stores the result on your device. You manage or cancel the subscription in the Google Play Store, and Google's handling of your purchase is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. What we do not collect

  • No use of your journal content for advertising, profiling or marketing.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of our own. Crash reports (section 2.5) are our only diagnostics; advertising data is described in section 3.
  • No GPS or precise device location.
  • No contacts, calendar, call logs, SMS or microphone access.
  • No payment card details.
  • No sale or rental of your personal information to data brokers.

6. How your information is used

Your information is used only to:

  • sign you in and keep your journal separate from every other user's;
  • save, display, search and sync your entries, mood logs and images;
  • draw your mood chart and calendar;
  • show a daily reminder notification, if you turn one on;
  • produce a text or PDF export when you ask for one;
  • provide My Diary Pro and apply the free-plan limits;
  • show ads in the free version (section 3);
  • diagnose and fix crashes.

We do not read your journal entries. We do not use your content to train machine-learning models. We do not use it for marketing or advertising.

If you are in the UK or EEA, our lawful bases for processing are: performance of a contract with you (providing the app and any subscription you bought); your consent for personalised advertising and for optional features such as reminders; and our legitimate interest in showing non-personalised ads to fund the free version, preventing ad fraud, and fixing crashes. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings.

7. Where your data is stored

Your entries, mood logs and backed-up images are stored in Google Cloud Firestore, operated by Google. They are filed under a path unique to your account, and the app's security rules permit access only to that signed-in account.

Google operates data centres in several countries, so your data may be processed outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Google's transfer safeguards are described in its own documentation, linked below.

Your app settings are stored only in your device's app-private storage.

9. Retention and deletion

Your entries, mood logs and images are kept until you delete them. There is no automatic expiry.

You are in control of removal:

  • Delete a single entry — open it and choose delete.
  • Delete everything — Settings → Delete app data. This permanently removes every journal entry, every mood log and every stored image for your account, on the device and in the cloud. It cannot be undone.
  • Delete your account — Settings → Delete account. After you confirm with your Google account or your email password, this removes all of the above and your sign-in record, then clears the app's data from the device. It cannot be undone.
  • Log out — clears your name, PIN and biometric preference from the device. Your entries and images remain in your account and return when you sign back in.
  • Uninstall — removes everything held on the device. Your entries, mood logs and images remain in your account until you delete them as above.

If you no longer have the app installed, you can ask us to delete your account and all of its data — see how to request account deletion. Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days and then deleted automatically. Advertising data is retained by Google according to its own policies. Deleting your account does not cancel a Pro subscription; cancel it in the Google Play Store.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict or erase your personal information, and to object to its processing. The app is built so you can exercise most of these yourself, immediately:

  • Access and portability — Settings → Export data gives you every entry as a text or PDF file.
  • Correction — edit any entry, or change your display name in Settings.
  • Erasure — Settings → Delete app data, or Settings → Delete account to remove your account as well.
  • Advertising — Settings → Ad privacy choices (UK, EEA and Switzerland), your phone's ad settings, or a Pro subscription to remove ads.
  • Withdrawing consent — turn reminders off in Settings, change your ad choices as above, or revoke the app's access from your Google account settings.

For anything you cannot do in the app, contact us and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the UK or EEA and are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your local data protection authority.

11. Security

  • Data in transit between the app and Google's servers is encrypted with TLS.
  • Firestore security rules scope every read and write to your own account, so one user cannot reach another user's journal or images.
  • You can add a PIN, and optionally biometric unlock, to keep the app closed to anyone else holding your phone. Repeated wrong PINs lock entry for an increasing time.
  • The app blocks screenshots and hides its content in the recent-apps screen.
  • On the device, images are kept in app-private internal storage, which other apps cannot read.

Being straight with you: your entries are encrypted in transit and at rest on Google's infrastructure, but they are not end-to-end encrypted. That means they are not technically sealed from the service operator in the way a zero-knowledge app would be. No method of transmission or storage is ever completely secure.

12. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
