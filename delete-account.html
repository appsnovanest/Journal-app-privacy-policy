Delete your account

Last updated: 20 November 2026

From inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open My Diary and unlock it.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete account.
  3. Confirm, then choose the same Google account you signed in with — or, if you signed in with email, enter your password.

Your account is deleted straight away, together with all of its data. The app then returns to the sign-in screen.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, email us from the address you used to sign in (your Google account, or the email you registered with) and ask us to delete your account:

appsnovanest@gmail.com

Please use the subject line “Delete my account”. We will confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in record (email address, display name and user ID).
  • Every journal entry, including titles, text, tags, places and favourites.
  • Every mood log.
  • Every photo, cover image and drawing backed up to the cloud.
  • When deleted from the app: everything the app stored on that phone, including your PIN and reminder settings.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. If you want a copy first, use Settings → Export data in the app before deleting.

What may be kept

Nothing that identifies you. Anonymous crash reports collected by Firebase Crashlytics are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically after 90 days. Advertising data collected by Google AdMob in the free version is not linked to your account and is kept by Google under its own policies. Deleting your account does not cancel a My Diary Pro subscription — cancel it in the Google Play Store. See the privacy policy for details.
